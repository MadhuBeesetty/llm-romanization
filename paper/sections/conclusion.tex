\section{Conclusion}

We presented a preliminary evaluation of Qwen3.5-35B for paired
English-to-Romanized Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada generation. BGE-M3 similarity
suggests that successful Telugu outputs preserve source semantics well, while
Hindi provides the strongest completion coverage. Kannada demonstrates that a
high conditional score can coexist with substantial generation failure.
Future evaluation should combine automatic semantic metrics with coverage,
format compliance, repetition detection, and native-speaker judgments.
