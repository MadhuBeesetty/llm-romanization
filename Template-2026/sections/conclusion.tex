\section{Conclusion}

We presented a preliminary evaluation of Qwen3.5-35B for paired
English-to-Romanized Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada generation. Under a
failure-aware policy that assigns zero to English copies and missing or
unusable fields, Hindi achieves the strongest aggregate semantic scores,
followed by Telugu and Kannada. Future evaluation should combine automatic
semantic metrics with completion and format diagnostics, repetition detection,
and native-speaker judgments.
